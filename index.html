A private publishing tool for my own channels

Newsdesk is a desktop tool made and used only by Fernando Valiente Dies (Sydney, Australia). It turns scripts I write into videos and publishes them, in English and Spanish, to my own YouTube channels: daily news Shorts and animated what-if, science and motivation stories.

It uses the YouTube API Services to upload my videos and to set their thumbnails, subtitles, playlists, publish times and one comment, and to read and reply to comments on my own videos. It has no other users and no sign-up.

This tool publishes through the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
