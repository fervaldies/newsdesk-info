Newsdesk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Newsdesk is a private publishing tool run by Fernando Valiente Dies (Sydney, Australia). It is used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What Newsdesk does with YouTube

Newsdesk uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own channels and to set their title, description, thumbnail, subtitles, playlist, publish time and one comment. It does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users or other channels.

By using YouTube features through Newsdesk you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data stored

  • OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret and refresh tokens) for the owner's own channels, stored only in a private file on the owner's computer and in private GitHub Secrets. They are never shared or published.
  • The IDs and links of videos the owner has uploaded, and their basic public statistics (views, likes), kept to plan future posts.

No personal data of viewers or other people is collected, sold or shared.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Stored data is deleted within 30 days of revoking access or on request.
